Support

We read every message. Write to support@lumtidy.app and we'll get back to you.

Will Lumtidy delete anything without asking?

No. Only exact copies are pre-selected, and nothing is deleted until you review and confirm. Favorites are never suggested.

I deleted something by mistake

Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. Everything Lumtidy deletes stays there for 30 days and can be recovered.

Why hasn't my free space gone up?

iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. The space is freed when that album is emptied. Lumtidy shows how much is waiting there and can take you to it.

What happens to my original when I shrink a video?

Lumtidy saves a new, smaller copy with the same date, location, favorite status and albums. The original goes to Recently Deleted, so you can compare and recover it for 30 days.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is analysed on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy.

What's free and what's Pro?

Scanning, exact duplicates and screenshots are free forever. Pro unlocks similar shots, shrinking videos and huge photos, Live Photos to stills, files & cloud drives, chats, large videos, screen recordings, receipts, blurry photos and swipe mode.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Lumtidy Pro → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. You can also reach this from Lumtidy's Settings → Manage subscription.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Lumtidy → Settings → Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

How does cleaning iCloud Drive or Google Drive work?

In Lumtidy, open Files & cloud drives and choose a folder. For Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, install their app and turn it on under Locations in the Files app first. Lumtidy only reads the folders you pick. Copies are only selected for you when the contents are identical byte for byte. Deleted files go to Recently Deleted in Files (iCloud Drive) or to that service's own trash.
