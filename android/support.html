Support for Android

We read every message. Write to hello@lumtidy.app and we'll get back to you. Using an iPhone? See iPhone support.

Will Lumtidy delete anything without asking?

No. Only exact copies are pre-selected, and nothing is deleted until you review and confirm. Favorites are never suggested, and the last copy of a photo is always kept.

I deleted something by mistake

On Android 11 and later, Lumtidy moves items to the trash, where they stay for 30 days. Open your gallery app's trash (for example Google Photos → Collections → Trash, or Samsung Gallery → Recycle bin) and restore them. On Android 10 and earlier there is no trash, so Lumtidy asks you to confirm and the items are deleted permanently.

Why hasn't my free space gone up?

Items in the trash still take up space until it is emptied, automatically after 30 days. Lumtidy shows how much is waiting there, and “Empty now” frees it straight away.

What happens to my original when I shrink a video?

Lumtidy saves a new, smaller copy next to it with the same date, and moves the original to the trash, so you can compare and recover it for 30 days.

Lumtidy only sees some of my photos

On Android 14 and later you can give access to selected photos only. To let Lumtidy see everything, open Android Settings → Apps → Lumtidy → Permissions → Photos and videos → Always allow all.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is analysed on your phone, and the Android app doesn't even have internet access. See the Privacy Policy.

What's free and what's Pro?

Scanning, exact duplicates and screenshots are free forever. Pro unlocks similar shots, shrinking videos and huge photos, Motion Photos to stills, chats, large videos, screen recordings, receipts, blurry photos and swipe mode.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Google Play app → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Lumtidy Pro → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. You can also reach this from Lumtidy's Settings → Manage subscription.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Lumtidy → Settings → Restore purchases, with the same Google account you used to buy it selected in the Google Play app.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google. See Google Play's refund help.
