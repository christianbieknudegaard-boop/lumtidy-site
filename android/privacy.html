Privacy Policy for Android

Effective 24 November 2026 · Using an iPhone? See the iPhone privacy policy.

In short: Lumtidy works entirely on your phone. The Android app doesn't even have permission to use the internet, so your photos, videos and everything Lumtidy learns about them stay on your device. We have no servers, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Who we are

Lumtidy is an Android app that helps you clean up the photos and videos on your phone. In this policy “we” means the developer of Lumtidy. You can reach us at hello@lumtidy.app.

No internet access

Lumtidy for Android does not request the Android “internet” permission, so Android does not let the app connect to the internet. Some of the libraries we use (Google ML Kit and Google Play Billing) can collect anonymous usage statistics; we have switched that off, and without internet access the app couldn't send them anyway.

Your photos and videos

Lumtidy asks for access to your photos and videos so it can find duplicates, similar shots, large videos and other items you may want to remove or make smaller. On Android 14 and later you can choose to give access to selected photos only.

  • All analysis happens on your phone. Text and barcodes in screenshots, receipts and notes are recognised with Google ML Kit, which runs on your device and does not send images anywhere.
  • Your photos, videos, their contents, location or other metadata are never uploaded, copied to us, or shared with anyone.
  • To make later scans faster, Lumtidy stores a small cache of analysis results (such as image fingerprints, sharpness scores and the location stored in each photo) in the app's private storage. It is deleted if you choose “Start fresh” in Settings or uninstall the app, and it is excluded from Android backups and phone-to-phone transfers.
  • To find your trips, Lumtidy reads the location already stored in your photos (Android's “media location” permission) and compares them with each other on your phone. It never asks for your current location and never looks places up online.
  • When you “love” a photo in Rediscover or while swiping, Lumtidy marks it as a favorite in your phone's media library.
  • The Home screen widget shows your garden and how much space you can free. These numbers are shared from the app to the widget on your phone only.
  • Nothing is deleted or changed without your confirmation. On Android 11 and later, deleted items go to the trash, where you can recover them for 30 days. On Android 10 and earlier, Android has no trash, so Lumtidy asks you to confirm and the items are deleted permanently.
  • When Lumtidy makes a video, a huge photo or a Motion Photo smaller, it saves the new version next to the original, with the same date, and then moves the original to the trash.
  • Lumtidy only sees photos stored on your phone. If you use a cloud service such as Google Photos, that service works on its own, under its own privacy policy.

Data we collect

None. Lumtidy has no user accounts, does not collect personal data, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Settings and history

Your preferences, the photos you chose to keep in swipe mode and your cleanup history (dates and amount of space freed) are stored only on your phone. They are not included in Android's backup to Google Drive.

Notifications

If you turn on the monthly reminder, Android asks for permission to show notifications, and Lumtidy schedules a local notification on your phone. No push service or server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Google through Google Play. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Lumtidy only asks the Google Play app on your phone whether Pro is active. Google's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos – to find what you can tidy.
  • Media location – to read the location stored in your photos, for Trips.
  • Notifications – only if you turn on the monthly reminder.

Children

Lumtidy does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything Lumtidy stores by uninstalling the app. You are also welcome to contact us with any questions about privacy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change ever means data leaves your phone, we'll ask for your permission in the app first.
