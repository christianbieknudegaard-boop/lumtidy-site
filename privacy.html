Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · Using Android? See the Android privacy policy.

In short: Lumtidy works entirely on your iPhone. Your photos and videos never leave your device, we have no servers that receive your data, and we don't use analytics, ads or tracking.

Who we are

Lumtidy is an iPhone app that helps you clean up your photo library. In this policy “we” means the developer of Lumtidy. You can reach us at hello@lumtidy.app.

Your photos and videos

Lumtidy asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, large videos and other items you may want to remove or make smaller.

  • All analysis happens on your iPhone using Apple's built-in frameworks (Photos and Vision).
  • Your photos, videos, their contents, location or other metadata are never uploaded, copied to us, or shared with anyone.
  • To make later scans faster, Lumtidy stores a small cache of analysis results (such as image fingerprints and sharpness scores) in the app's private storage on your device. It is deleted if you choose “Start fresh” in Settings or delete the app.
  • To find your trips, Lumtidy compares the location already stored in your photos with each other, on your iPhone. It never asks for your current location and never looks places up online. When you “love” a photo in Rediscover, Lumtidy marks it as a favorite and adds it to an album called Rediscovered in your library.
  • The Home Screen widget shows your garden and how much space you can free. These numbers are shared from the app to the widget on your device only.
  • Nothing is deleted or changed without your confirmation. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, your iPhone may download previews from Apple's iCloud while scanning. This happens between your device and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Data we collect

None. Lumtidy has no user accounts, does not collect personal data, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Settings and history

Your preferences, the photos you chose to keep in swipe mode and your cleanup history (dates and amount of space freed) are stored only on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the monthly reminder, Lumtidy schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Lumtidy only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Pro is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Children

Lumtidy does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything Lumtidy stores by deleting the app. You are also welcome to contact us with any questions about privacy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change ever means data leaves your device, we'll ask for your permission in the app first.
